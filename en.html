Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026 · العربية

In short

Laqta makes your film entirely on your phone. Your photos and videos are never sent to a server, and we collect no data about you or how you use the app.

Data we collect

None. There is no account or sign-in, no analytics, no tracking, no ads, and no crash reports sent to us.

Your photos and videos

  • The app reads the photos and videos you pick to analyse them (sharpness, light, faces, duplicates) and make a film from them. All of this happens on the device.
  • Analysis results are cached on your device only, to make the next film faster. You can clear them in Settings › “Clear temporary files”.
  • We never modify or delete your originals. Films are saved as new files.
  • No photo, video, audio, location (GPS), face data or file name is uploaded anywhere.

Permissions and why

Photos and videos
To let you pick your memories and make the film. You can grant access to selected items only (limited access); the app works normally.
Save to gallery
To save finished films to the “Laqta” album. You can turn this off in Settings.
Notifications (Android)
To show export progress while you are outside the app. If you decline, the export still runs, without a notification.

Sharing

When you tap “Share”, the film is handed to the app you choose (WhatsApp, TikTok, Instagram…) through the operating system. What happens next is governed by that app's privacy policy.

Ratings

“Rate Laqta” opens the Google Play or App Store rating prompt, governed by the store's policies.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, an optional feature that needs the internet), we will update this page and its date. Anything that sends data will be optional and clearly explained before it runs.

Contact

Privacy questions: open an issue here.
